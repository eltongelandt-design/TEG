Reference ranges and rules

Ranges are taken from the analyser screenshot and are editable. Changes are kept in this browser only. Check them against your analyser, assay and local protocol.

How the pathways are decided

  1. FFP: CK R above range. If CKH R is entered and within range, the prolonged CK R is flagged as a likely heparin effect instead.
  2. Cryo / fibrinogen: CFF MA below range.
  3. Platelets: CRT MA below range and CFF MA entered and within range. If CFF MA is low or missing, platelets are flagged for review only.
  4. TXA: CK LY30 above range (possible hyperfibrinolysis).
  5. Heparin effect (addition to the workbook): CK R above range with CKH R within range.

Prototype, not a validated clinical decision rule. It transcribes a teaching diagram and does not prescribe treatment. Interpret alongside bleeding status, clinical findings and other laboratory results. Product choice, dose, contraindications, repeat testing and escalation follow local policy and the responsible clinician. Do not use as the sole basis for transfusion or medication decisions.
